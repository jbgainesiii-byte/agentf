Three case patterns, three ways Agentforce handles them

Same platform, different operating modes depending on the case type. The live demo walks through one of each.
Strong fit

Transfer of Ownership

Agentforce intakes, classifies, requests documents, routes to human for approval. Routine work removed, human owns the decision.

Draft for review

Drive system warning (safety)

Agentforce detects, routes immediately to senior agent, drafts a proposed response. Nothing automated goes to the customer. Human edits and sends.

Full resolution

Mobile app question

Common question grounded in the knowledge base. Agentforce answers and closes the case on its own. No human touch needed.
